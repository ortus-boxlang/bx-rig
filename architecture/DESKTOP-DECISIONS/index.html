    Desktop decisions

    Owner decisions on the desktop shell (top bar, dock, icons, themes, helpers). Status is decided or planned.

    On this page

    Desktop decisions (owner, 2026-11-17)

    Status: decided means the owner chose it; planned means it is agreed and scheduled but not built yet.

    Top bar and dock

    DecisionChoiceStatus
    Top bar layout (wizard)Asks Minimal, Standard (default, recommended) or Dense, with a preview in the TUIdecided
    Minimal layoutKeeps workspaces, active window and quick settingsdecided
    DockON by default; the Launchpad grid is its first button; the grid leaves the bar when the dock is ondecided
    ClockOne widget showing Nov 5 - 04:00 (format is user-configurable); its popover shows other zones and a route to the calendar; clock and world clock are mergeddecided
    BatteryShows the percentage, and only on laptopsdecided
    Clipboard historyTop right of the bardecided
    Last two iconsQuick settings, then control center (control center is last)decided
    CaffeineA switch inside quick settingsdecided
    Quick settingsThe bxrig/quick plugin (evolved from the theme switcher): a popover of switches and pickers (Caffeine, Do Not Disturb, Dark/Light/Auto, Dock, Battery percentage, App grid style, Top bar layout, Clock format, Night light note, Control Center button), with the theme list and preview as its second page (Themes row)decided
    Battery and powerOne panel: the built-in battery widget stays; left click opens the bxrig/power panel (profile, lid on battery and AC, Suspend, Hibernate, charge limit when supported), right click the control center Power tab; the power_profile widget stays off the bardecided
    Brightness, weatherNoctalia built-ins, not on the bardecided
    VPNIts own bar icon; NordVPN, NordLayer, Tailscale, Mullvad and NetworkManager profiles in one pick-one list with data-driven adapters; an allow-several toggle (built: bxrig vpn, plugin bxrig/vpn; the look is checked on screen later)decided
    System popoverUpdates and Backups (bxrig/system: bar glyph with the waiting-update count, tabs Updates and Backups; built, visual check pending)decided
    Developer panelSeparate from System: databases, containers, VMs (plugin bxrig/dev, bxrig containers list\|start\|stop\|restart\|logs)decided
    Capture iconOn Standard and Densedecided
    Noctalia control centerReused, not extended (tabs cannot be added); only the screen-time tab is hiddendecided
    Panel keysSuper+Ctrl+A/B/N/P/D fixed (they open the control center)decided
    Hardware menu (Super+Ctrl+H)Keptdecided

    Icons, apps and themes

    DecisionChoiceStatus
    Icon style on the barHybrid: bare Tabler outline glyphs colored by one palette role; plates only on the logo button and on the Launchpad grid in the dock (plates need an outline or glow on dark and Retro)decided
    Icon themeBxRig, style A (superellipse tile, vertical glow, Phosphor duotone, MIT notice), new bxrig-* names, Inherits=Papirus-Dark; our icon where we have one, else Papirus; the icon set is not asked in the wizard. Built: tools/gen-icon-theme.py (31 icons, 16 to 256 px, bold darker variant at 16 and 22 px), bxrig appearance icons bxrig\|papirus\|status, applied automatically by initdecided, built
    App gridDefault Launchpad; a second style, Terminal-style grid, is selectable in quick settings via appearance appgridplanned
    Theme selectionA separate, bigger TUI selector plus a wizard Theme page (default BxRig); the Look page has no theme list (built: bxrig tui themes, list with swatches, filters, wallpaper, palette and sample terminal preview, r live preview; the wizard page keeps its own list)decided
    Retro themebxrig-retro (80s synthwave, neon magenta #ff2ec4) with Share Tech Mono Nerd (ttf-sharetech-mono-nerd, named by font = "sharetech" in its colors.toml); wallpaper drawn by tools/gen-retro-wallpaper.py; bxrig banner is its ASCII greeting. Not done: a starship accent and wiring the banner into the starter shelldecided
    Wizard colorsThe wizard follows the chosen theme (TuiKit must stop hard-coding the Frappe palette)planned
    Noctalia plugin storeThe official store is not used; pinned own plugins onlydecided
